Fusion Explainers

First-principles physics and economics of fusion energy. Every D-T fusion reaction releases a 14 MeV neutron, and much of the interesting engineering is about what you do with it: breed tritium, multiply it, or turn it into something you can sell. The math is shown, not skipped.

Fusion Gold: Mercury to Gold

A fusion plant has to multiply its neutrons to breed fuel. Make the multiplier mercury-198 and each multiplication leaves one atom of gold behind. The physics works; one unproven number decides whether it pays.
